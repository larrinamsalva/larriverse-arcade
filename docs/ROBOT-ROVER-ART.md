# Robot Rover terrain and movement

All 80 authored worlds use local pictures for the meadow, canyon, crystal, and cosmic settings. Four views of the same wheeled rover show its north, east, south, and west facing. Matching rock pictures, a star beacon, terrain textures, and an exploration case complete the environments.

The 19 new WebP images total about 578 KiB. Backgrounds live in `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/rover-*.webp`, the two-pose introduction character in `assets/world-motion/characters/rover-explorer.webp`, its case in `assets/world-motion/objects/rover-sample-case.webp`, and game objects and surfaces in `games/robot-rover/art/`.

The code runner captures the rover's current rendered position, then animates its new picture from that position to the next actual grid cell. This keeps travel aligned with the 5-by-5 board when the viewport or text size changes. The existing command engine continues to own direction, collision checks, program pacing, scoring, and progression. Motion ends immediately when either the site's comfort preference or device reduced motion is enabled. The direction arrow, facing text, native grid labels, and visited-path markers remain available during static play.

Browser coverage checks actual cell-to-cell travel, all four facing pictures, keyboard commands, interruption by both motion preferences, and controls at 320 pixels with 200 percent text. The complete campaign test also verifies that all four backgrounds and direction pictures load across 80 worlds, along with collision feedback, ranks, stars, local completion, and replay.

## Original generation prompts

The images were created with the built-in image generation tool. The shipped atlas cells were cropped, resized, and encoded as WebP, preserving generated alpha. Two rover cells also form the introduction's two-pose sheet.

### landscapes

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production landscape atlas for LarriVerse Robot Rover, a children's coding adventure. A 2 by 2 grid of four separate wide cinematic 3D animated-feature landscapes, equal panels, straight edge-to-edge divisions, no gutters or frames. Every panel has a clear empty foreground ground plane across its bottom half for a game board, with the setting in the upper half. Top left MEADOW: welcoming green meadow research outpost, wooden footpath, rounded trees and distant hills, small cream-and-teal science habitat, clear morning sky. Top right CANYON: sunlit red sandstone canyon, layered buttes, ochre sandy clearing and tiny distant exploration shelter, warm light and pale sky. Bottom left CRYSTAL: spacious lavender crystal cavern, believable faceted amethyst and quartz formations at the sides, softly glowing mineral light, flat violet-gray stone foreground with room to explore. Bottom right COSMIC: a quiet moon outpost, charcoal gray regolith, low distant white habitat and antenna, stars and a large softly lit ringed planet in a deep blue sky. Camera: slightly raised eye level, clear natural perspective, consistent with tabletop toy worlds. Beautiful detailed textures, atmospheric depth, warm and welcoming family animation aesthetic, recognizable ground and architecture. No people, robots, vehicles, animals, grids, arrows, letters, numbers, logos, UI or watermarks. Keep all four horizons in the upper half and the foreground clear. Each panel should read at 832 by 468 pixels.

### rover

One 2 by 2 grid of FOUR equal square cells, actual transparent alpha background, no checkerboard. Show the SAME friendly small six-wheel exploration rover in four compass directions, complete and centered with generous margins in every cell. Design: rounded cream-white body with teal protective panels, a dark glass face with two bright cyan expressive eyes on the FRONT, visible rubber-tread wheels and believable suspension, a tiny orange antenna and a dark blue solar panel on top, brushed metal joints and small fasteners. A wheeled robot vehicle, no humanoid legs or arms. Fixed elevated 35-degree camera looking down onto a flat map, natural 3D animated-feature materials, warm neutral upper-left lighting, no ground plane except tiny self shadow, no text or symbols. Top left NORTH: the rover drives away toward the top edge, show its back, front eyes hidden. Top right EAST: rover faces the right edge, with its front glass face on the right end. Bottom left SOUTH: rover drives toward the bottom edge, show the front with the two cyan eyes. Bottom right WEST: rover faces the left edge, front glass face on the left end. These are four yaw rotations of one identical rover, NOT rotated or mirrored copies of the entire photograph. Keep its shape, wheels, antenna, colors, scale and center consistent. Entire tires and antenna must fit inside each cell, maximum 78 percent of each cell, clean separate silhouettes. No scenery, compass arrows, frames, writing, logos or watermarks.
