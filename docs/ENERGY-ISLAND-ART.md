# Energy Island artwork and motion

Energy Island uses sixteen illustrated landscapes: coast, village, forest and mountain, each with sunny, cloudy, night and breezy weather. A two-pose technician welcomes the player. Recognizable solar panels, wind turbines, closed battery cabinets and a cottage replace the emoji equipment scene.

The 23 new local WebP files total about 1.29 MiB. Landscapes are 832 × 468 in `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/energy-{zone}-{weather}.webp`. The technician is a 640 × 320 two-pose sheet at `assets/world-motion/characters/energy-technician.webp`. The 256 × 256 solar array is `assets/world-motion/objects/energy-solar-panel.webp`; the five other 256 × 256 parts are in `games/energy-island/art/`. The scene and builder share these assets.

## Learning and animation behavior

Before testing, the scene is explicitly a build preview. After each test it shows the last logged day's weather, while the forecast highlights the next day. The cottage windows glow only when that logged day met demand. Native text gives the result, production and demand outside the artwork. The battery bar uses stored units divided by the exact built capacity; no batteries gives 0 / 0 and an empty bar.

Installed pictures match every purchased unit, including larger builds. Only turbine rotors turn; the towers stay still. Rotor speed follows the toy forecast's wind value. Solar glints stop at night. Both the site's Reduce motion setting and the operating-system preference stop equipment animation, while leaving results visible. The technician's pose animation uses the existing shared scene player.

All sixty level definitions, prices, forecasts, energy arithmetic, token limits, scoring, rewards and completion storage retain their existing rules. Unfinished energy boards are not saved. Native keyboard controls and labels remain; focus returns to the selected builder button after a rerender. The four-column log has a keyboard-scrollable region for narrow screens and enlarged text. The skip link hides by its actual height rather than a fixed offset, so wrapped enlarged text stays off the board until the link receives focus. Compact spacing leaves room for storage labels at 320 pixels and 200% text.

## Artwork provenance and production brief

Seven original atlases/sheets were created with the built-in image generator, then deterministically cropped, transparently padded where needed, resized and encoded as local WebP. The generated alpha is preserved. There are no external asset requests, added dependencies or generated text/measurements. Artwork is distributed under the repository's existing MIT license.

Background brief: four separate 2 × 2 atlases, one per zone, with the same camera and landscape in sunny (top left), cloudy (top right), night (bottom left), and breezy (bottom right) light. Detailed cinematic 3D animated-feature environments, natural materials, high horizon, empty level grassy foreground for separate sprites. Coast: turquoise bay, wildflowers, shore and rolling hills. Village: distant cream cottages, red roofs and garden paths. Forest: trees, pines and a sheltered blue inlet. Mountain: alpine flowers, rocky slopes, distant peaks and blue water. Night uses actual moonlight and stars; breezy uses leaning grasses and branches. No people, animals, equipment, prominent foreground houses, writing, UI, labels or logos.

### Technician prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: two-pose character sprite sheet for LarriVerse Energy Island, a children's fictional power-planning game. Primary request: a friendly adult technician with medium brown skin, short dark hair, orange safety helmet, lime safety vest, navy overalls and brown work boots, holding a blank clipboard in his left hand. Detailed polished cinematic 3D animated-feature illustration with believable anatomy, natural face, proper hands and five fingers. Composition: wide sheet of TWO equal square cells side by side on true alpha transparency. Same character, camera, proportions, scale and baseline in both. Left cell: relaxed standing pose smiling; right cell: small welcoming wave with right hand, clipboard still in left. Entire full body visible, feet at about 86 percent cell height, generous clear margins, centered in each cell. No scenery, pedestal, floor, background shadow, writing, logos, labels, border or watermark. Actual transparent background.

### Equipment prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2 by 2 square-cell transparent equipment atlas for LarriVerse Energy Island children's fictional power-planning game. Detailed polished cinematic 3D animated-feature rendered objects, recognizable materials, each complete and isolated centered within its own equal square cell, generous transparent margins. TOP LEFT: one small blue photovoltaic solar-panel array tilted on a low metal stand, elevated front three-quarter view, visible frame and blue cell grid, no sun symbol. TOP RIGHT: one closed gray-and-teal home battery cabinet standing on floor, blank dark display, no wires or exposed terminals. BOTTOM LEFT: FRONT VIEW wind turbine TOWER ONLY, white tapered pole with nacelle and small round hub, hub precisely at 50 percent cell width and 35 percent cell height, base at 87 percent cell height, NO BLADES, clear open space around hub. BOTTOM RIGHT: FRONT VIEW ROTOR ONLY, exactly three white gently curved aerodynamic turbine blades radiating from a small round central hub exactly at center of square, tips fully inside cell, generous margins, NO TOWER. Rotor hub and tower hub match in design, separately composited and animated later. True alpha background. No text, numbers, symbols, logos, scenery, ground, border, diagram or watermark.

### House states prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: paired house states sprite sheet for LarriVerse Energy Island children's fictional power-planning game. Detailed cinematic 3D animated-feature look with believable materials. Wide sheet TWO equal square cells side by side on actual alpha transparency. Both cells show EXACTLY the same small cream-colored cottage with red roof tiles, brown front door, two front windows, little stone doorstep and tiny grass patch around base. Elevated front three-quarter view, same framing, architecture, camera, lighting, scale, baseline and position. Left cell: both windows DARK and unlit, doorway unlit. Right cell: both windows warmly illuminated golden yellow with soft warm doorway glow; no change to house itself. Complete roof and doorstep within cell, generous clear margins. No other objects, people, power cables, sky, scenery, text, labels, symbols, logos, borders, frames or watermark. Transparent background; no floor beyond small grass base.

## Verification

`npm run validate` checks the repository contracts. Focused browser checks in `tests/browser/energy-island-art.spec.mjs` exercise actual blade movement with stationary towers, keyboard building/clear/token rejection, successful and failed power states, night production, both reduced-motion preferences, contrast modes and 320-pixel layout at 200% text size.

The sixty-level Energy Island test in `tests/browser/skill-worlds.spec.mjs` checks all sixteen landscape/weather combinations, exact installed counts, the last logged day's scene, capacity and storage labels, 180-star completion, replay and saved best scores on desktop and phone. Shared world-motion tests cover local intro layers. Screenshot review is separate from these functional checks; tests do not establish aesthetic quality.
