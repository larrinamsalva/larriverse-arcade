# Garden Guardians artwork

The 60 planting challenges use the four authored zones, with a local illustrated background for each meadow, orchard, hill, and sunset garden. Carrot, bean, and flower pictures each have three growth stages. Soil and timber textures frame the six labelled native plot buttons.

New assets are stored in `games/garden-guardians/art/`, `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/guardian-*.webp`, and `assets/world-motion/characters/guardian-robin.webp`. The existing `assets/world-motion/objects/garden-grow-watering-can.webp` is shared with Garden Grow & Harvest. The 16 new WebP assets total about 697 KiB. Generated alpha is preserved in the crop and character images.

The robin has two aligned poses. Planting and watering animate the changed plot, grown plants sway gently, and the watering can appears above the plant's label. Written crop names, growth fractions, water totals, and watered-plot borders provide feedback alongside motion. Both the site comfort setting and the device reduced-motion setting stop these effects.

These are the game's existing simplified growth stages and water units. The artwork does not change the planting plans, quotas, water allowances, scoring, completion records, or replay.

## Original generation prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Only atlas cropping, resizing, and WebP encoding were applied after generation.

### settings

In each panel keep the bottom 45 percent an open compacted-earth garden path / building clearing to layer interactive raised beds in the game; distant foliage, beds and buildings occupy the upper half. Top left: fresh morning meadow community garden, rolling green field, cottage and wooden fence, tiny wildflower borders, a pale blue sky. Top right: leafy orchard garden with apple trees and a rustic garden shed, dappled afternoon light, grass paths. Bottom left: sunny hillside terrace garden with low stone retaining walls, distant rolling hills, soft high clouds, ochre paths. Bottom right: the same kind of welcoming garden at golden sunset, peach-orange sky, long soft shadows, low timber fence and warm foliage. Style: high quality believable 3D animated film scenery, detailed natural leaves, soil, wood and stone, clean rich forms, realistic perspective rather than flat vectors. Keep all four scenes distinct and legible when reduced to 832 by 468 pixels. No painted plants or objects in the foreground clearing.
