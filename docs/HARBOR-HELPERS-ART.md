# Harbor Helpers island deliveries

All 80 routes now use one of eight local island settings chosen from the authored island's icon: gardens and orchards, workshops, lighthouse beacons, learning centers, community islands, mountain landings, markets, or festivals. Eight distinct transport pictures follow the game's eight transport stages from skiff to grand ferry convoy. All ten cargo types have recognizable supply pictures in both the loading shelf and the unloadable manifest.

The 29 new WebP assets total about 1.1 MB. Settings are in `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/harbor-*.webp`, the two-pose worker in `assets/world-motion/characters/harbor-helper.webp`, the introduction prop in `assets/world-motion/objects/harbor-life-ring.webp`, and cargo, vessels and the mooring bollard in `games/harbor-helpers/art/`.

The boat bobs gently at the departure dock, then travels fully within the scene when a correct delivery is accepted. Both the site's comfort setting and device reduced motion stop the animation and show an accepted delivery at its arrival position. The native supply controls, visible names, loading counts, unload buttons, capacity rules, request matching and saved completion remain the basis of play. Island and transport names sit outside the decorative scene. The chapter label also reports the actual eight-chapter campaign.

Focused browser checks cover illustrated cargo loading/unloading, keyboard controls, a full boat, sailing, arrival bounds, both motion settings, dark/high contrast, and 320-pixel layouts with 200 percent text. The established campaign check also decodes every setting, transport and unlocked cargo image as it completes all 80 deliveries and checks saved completion and replay.

## Original generation prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Atlas cells were cropped and resized for the game, and the generated transparency was preserved. The ferry atlas uses its visible gaps as crop boundaries so the larger convoy remains intact. No generative edits or manual painting were applied after generation.
