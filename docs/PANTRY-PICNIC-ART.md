# Pantry Picnic artwork and motion

All 80 authored picnics now travel through four settings: a community garden, an orchard, a lakeside picnic, and a community courtyard. Every one of the 13 foods has its own recognizable picture on both the shelf and in the packed box. A two-pose picnic helper and an open wicker basket introduce the world on a clear garden path.

The 22 local WebP assets total about 883 KiB:
- Four 832 × 468 picnic landscapes and a separate garden welcome setting in `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/picnic-*.webp`.
- The two-pose 640 × 320 helper in `assets/world-motion/characters/picnic-helper.webp`.
- A 256 × 256 basket in `assets/world-motion/objects/picnic-basket.webp`.
- Thirteen 256 × 256 food pictures and two material tiles in `games/pantry-picnic/art/`.

The food pictures match the existing names: a bread sandwich, prepared beans, a rice cup, a veggie wrap, pasta salad, apple pieces, banana slices, orange wedges, mixed berries, a grape bunch, carrot sticks, cucumber rounds, and snap peas. Labels, available counts and use-first tags remain native text outside the artwork. Buttons retain their original packing, returning, capacity and validation behavior.

Adding a portion gives only that new portion a brief packing movement; returning food leaves the remaining portions still. Site and device reduced-motion preferences stop the packing and landscape effects while keeping the food fully visible. Native controls fit 320-pixel screens with enlarged text, and packed-food names sit beside their pictures on phones.

The eight chapters, stock and leftover rules, three-portion capacity, keyboard operation, checkpoint reload, one completion reward and confirmed replay remain in place. The existing pretend-food and adult-help wording remains available.

Focused browser checks cover actual packing movement, keyboard packing and returning, stock restoration, full-box feedback, both motion preferences, dark/high contrast, and 320 pixels with 200% text. Campaign checks complete all 80 picnics on desktop and phone, verify all 13 food pictures and four settings, reload a saved checkpoint and exercise completion and replay.

## Original generation prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The project assets use atlas cropping, resizing and WebP encoding only; generated alpha is preserved for the food, basket and helper. Background panels are fitted to their production dimensions.
