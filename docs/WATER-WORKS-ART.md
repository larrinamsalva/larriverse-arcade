# Water Works artwork and flow

The 80 authored pipe networks now travel through four illustrated settings: meadow villages, mountain valleys, orchard gardens, and coastal towns. Reservoir, town, and toy-filter pictures identify the three key positions beside the native diagram. Shrubs and trees fill unused ground cells, while a two-pose helper and rotating paddle wheel introduce the world.

The 13 new WebP assets total about 610 KiB. Landscapes live in `assets/world-motion/backgrounds/water-*.webp`, the character in `assets/world-motion/characters/water-helper.webp`, the wheel in `assets/world-motion/objects/water-paddle-wheel.webp`, and equipment, foliage and material textures in `games/water-works/art/`. The existing Garden Guardians soil texture is shared for the garden ground cells.

Pipes remain exact native SVG geometry with the authored north/east/south/west ports. Brushed-steel texture, curved elbows and bolted end fittings follow those ports. The traced route supplies each pipe's incoming side, so animated water moves toward the next connection even when a straight pipe's stored pair is reversed. Number badges sit below the drawings, keeping bends and the filter marker clear with enlarged text. The original filter marker, numbered controls, keyboard rotation, leak feedback, route checker, rank progression, completion records, and toy-water safety wording remain available. Site and device reduced motion stop the movement while the connected route stays visibly filled.

Focused browser coverage checks keyboard rotation, leaking and connected states, actual changing dash positions, flow direction, both motion preferences, dark/high contrast and enlarged text at 320 pixels. Campaign coverage completes all 80 networks on desktop and phone and checks incoming flow directions and all four settings.

## Original generation prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Only atlas cropping, resizing, and WebP encoding were applied, preserving generated alpha for equipment, foliage and the character. Precise pipe diagrams use native code and the generated material texture.

### settings

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production landscape atlas for LarriVerse Water Works, a children's pipe-connection learning game. Create one 2 by 2 grid of FOUR equal wide landscape paintings, edge-to-edge panels with no gutters or borders. Rich believable cinematic 3D animated-feature environment art, natural lighting, detailed water, timber, concrete, stone and foliage, friendly and welcoming. Each panel shows a small reservoir on the LEFT and a community on the RIGHT, linked by the idea of a water network, with no visible pipe diagram. A clear dry gravel clearing fills the bottom 35 percent for the game's separate interactive board. No foreground objects in that clearing. Top left MEADOW: a round stone reservoir beside a gentle stream, leafy trees and colorful cottage village, warm sunny day. Top right MOUNTAIN: clean alpine reservoir among pine trees and distant mountain peaks, small timber village and water tower on the right, crisp morning. Bottom left ORCHARD: reservoir beside an orchard and vegetable gardens, a small farming village with red roofs on the right, soft afternoon sunlight. Bottom right COAST: calm coastal water-storage pond and a welcoming seaside town with a light blue water tower, evening golden light, gentle ocean in the distance. Keep all important architecture away from the very top and side edges, horizon near the upper third, restrained perspective. No people, vehicles, animals, arrows, grid, letters, labels, logos, UI or watermark. Each panel should remain legible at 832 by 468 pixels.
