# Bubble Resonance painted chapter settings

Four original, local landscape illustrations now sit behind the transparent bubble canvas: Sunlit Cove for levels 1–20, Moonlit Garden for 21–40, Aurora Valley for 41–60, and Starlit Observatory for 61–80. They are ambient settings, independent of the randomly generated grid and shot outcome. A text strip names the current setting while the scene image itself is decorative.

The canvas keeps its numbered bubbles, aim guide, danger line, keyboard and touch controls, sound setting, score, rewards, and eighty-level progression. A translucent canvas surface tones down the art in dark and light themes; high contrast hides the painting and uses a black play surface. The artwork has no round game pieces or numerical clues.

Review desktop and 320px mobile screenshots at the chapter boundaries for bubble and number visibility, aim path contrast, text legibility, image crop, and whether the four settings feel distinct. The screenshots are visual evidence, not a substitute for playing an entire run.
