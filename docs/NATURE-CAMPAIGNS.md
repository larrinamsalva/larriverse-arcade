# Garden Guardians and Energy Island: Eight-level campaigns

These upgrades deepen **two existing LarriVerse cabinets** without adding a new game,
changing the arcade's local-only record system, or relaxing human release approval.

## Garden Guardians — eight gardens, four ranks

Each stage has a unique name, planting ratio, illustration palette, water allowance,
and clear harvest goal. Players place six carrots, beans, and/or flower plots, water
each no more than once per day, and learn that each crop needs two separate watering
days in this *simplified toy*. Twelve drops let all six grow; ten or eleven drops
encourage prioritizing at least five.

Ranks: **Sprout Starter, Soil Scout, Pollinator Partner, Garden Steward**.
An ideal level earns three stars for growing the target and following the planting
plan, two for meeting the target with a different mix, and one for experimentation.
The next garden always unlocks, without penalty or time pressure.

## Energy Island — eight changing power grids

Four-day forecasts vary by level; builder token budgets grow from 12 to 18,
and daily energy demand grows from 6 to 8 fictional units. Players build toy solar
panels (2 tokens), turbines (3 tokens), and batteries (2 tokens, four-unit storage).
The rules intentionally simplify real electricity and weather.

Ranks: **Power Beginner, Weather Watcher, Storage Planner, Island Engineer**.
Stages award three stars for four fully powered days, two for three days, and one
for a different outcome. Players can clear their plan before starting and study
the energy ledger after each day. Every scenario is independently solvable within
its budget.

## Safety, comfort, and release

- All play is local to the browser, with no new tracking or accounts.
- Audio remains off until opted in; theme, contrast, reduced motion, Bloom, and
  keyboard-accessible native buttons retain the existing shared controls.
- The new stage data is part of the repository and validated for solvability.
- Automated browser checks play every level and verify progress and scores.
- Manual review of artwork, real devices, keyboard, touch, contrast, and game feel
  remains mandatory; automated evidence does not count as owner approval.
