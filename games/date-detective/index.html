Grown-up guide

What this world teaches

Most packaged-food dates in the U.S. are quality guidance rather than a universal federal safety cutoff. Infant formula is an important exception: FDA requires a Use By date and says not to use formula after it. Date-label laws can also vary by state, and manufacturer lot codes are not one universal system.
