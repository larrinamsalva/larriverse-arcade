For families, teachers & curious explorers

Practice is not an emergency plan

The anime-style illustrations are fictional teaching pictures, not medical photographs, emergency instructions or first-aid certification. Children should practice with adults. Situations can change quickly, and the correct real-world response depends on the person and surroundings. Check the scene, call for trained help when needed, and give care only as appropriate to your training. U.S. Poison Help is 1-800-222-1222.
