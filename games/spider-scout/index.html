Look with your eyes. Leave spiders alone.

Most spiders avoid humans; many help control insects. Some, including widow and recluse spiders in the U.S., can cause serious bites. A picture or skin mark cannot confirm species or a bite. Never pick one up to check.

Science and spider safety

These original SVG drawings show general body shapes and common habitats. Spiders can vary in appearance by sex, age, species, and geography. “Venomous” is the better word for biting spiders. We cannot certify a spider as safe or dangerous from an illustration alone.
