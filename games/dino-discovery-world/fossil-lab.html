🔬 The science behind the fun

Body fossils preserve remains, trace fossils preserve signs of activity, and plant impressions preserve clues about ancient vegetation. Finding a fossil does not automatically tell us the species, age, colors or behavior. Real fossil collection often needs professional permission—leave unknown fossils where they are and ask a responsible adult or museum educator.
