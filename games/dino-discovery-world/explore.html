🌍 Exploring with real science

Every scene is a simplified 3D model for learning—not a precise reconstruction. Habitats, colors, sounds and animal behavior are sometimes uncertain. We separate each geologic period so no Jurassic Stegosaurus shares a scene with Late Cretaceous T. rex. Birds are the only dinosaurs living today.

For further reading, explore the American Museum of Natural History, the Natural History Museum's dinosaur directory and the Dino Discovery World museum and science missions.

No GPS, sign-in, trackers, outside 3D model downloads, ads or purchases. No time limits, punishments or forced exploration.
