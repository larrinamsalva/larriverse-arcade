Detective rules

1

Find the words. Look for phrases like Best if Used By, Sell By, or Freeze By.

2

Don’t decode mystery numbers. Lot and batch codes can be manufacturer-specific.

3

Check the package too. Open, leaking, bulging, torn, or broken-seal packages need adult attention.

4

Bread colors vary. This game uses one sample bakery chart so you can practice reading a chart.

Important: Food dates alone do not prove that a food is safe. Follow storage/cooking directions and ask a trusted adult when unsure. Infant formula has special federal date requirements.
